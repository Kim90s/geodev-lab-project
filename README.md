# GeoDev Lab Africa Project - Wammakko Health Access Analysis

## Research Question
Which wards in Wammakko Local Government Area have settlements that sit more than 5 km away from a primary healthcare facility?
Built over twelve months with GeoDev Lab Africa. See `project-brief.md` for full data links and project brief.
## Key Findings & Answer
While eastern Wammakko enjoys heavy health facility coverage with overlapping 5 km buffers, large clusters of rural settlements in the **central, western, and northern wards** sit completely outside the 5 km catchment area, revealing significant spatial inequality in healthcare access.
