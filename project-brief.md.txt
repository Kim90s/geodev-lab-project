My GeoDev Lab Project Brief

1. The Question
Which wards in Wammakko Local Government Area have settlements that sit more than 5 km away from a primary healthcare facility?

2. Why It Matters
Local public health planners and state ministries can use this physical accessibility gap analysis to identify underserved rural settlements, prioritize emergency transport routes, and allocate mobile clinic resources efficiently.

3. Data Required
- Operational Ward Boundaries for Wammakko LGA (Vector Polygons)
- Settlement Extents / Locations in Wammakko LGA (Vector Points/Polygons)
- Health Facility Points in Wammakko LGA (Vector Points)

4. Data Sources & Links
- Ward Boundaries: GRID3 Nigeria (NGA Operational Wards v3.0) — https://data.grid3.org — GeoPackage — ~15 MB
- Settlement Extents: GRID3 Nigeria (NGA Settlement Extents v4.1) — https://data.grid3.org — GeoPackage — ~40 MB
- Health Facilities: GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities v3.0 / OpenStreetMap via QuickOSM — https://data.grid3.org — GeoPackage — ~5 MB

5. What I Will Build
A spatial processing pipeline and automated accessibility dashboard view in QGIS that runs distance-buffer calculations and generates summary maps whenever updated health facility or ward datasets are loaded.
